### Decision
Avoid creating layers of summaries.

**Why**
Repeatedly summarizing research risks flattening nuanced findings into generic advice. Instead of creating summaries of summaries, I will reference the original research whenever making design decisions.

Research should remain the source of truth rather than being continuously condensed.

**Status**
🔒 Locked

---

### Decision
Treat research as a reference library rather than a document to memorize.

**Why**
The purpose of the research is to support future design decisions, not to produce another deliverable. As I move into design, I'll revisit the relevant reports when a specific decision needs supporting evidence.

**Status**
🔒 Locked

### Decision
Design exploration is the next phase—not visual design.

**Why**
Before writing a PRD or opening Figma, I want to understand the landscape of successful portfolio experiences.

This phase focuses on studying:
- homepage structure
- storytelling
- writing
- navigation
- motion
- interaction patterns
- layout
- typography

The goal is not to copy visuals but to understand how successful portfolios solve design problems.

**Status**
🔒 Locked

### Decision
Document observations—not critiques.

**Why**
The goal isn't to review other portfolios.

Instead, I want to capture design decisions that caught my attention.

Good:
"Hero establishes credibility before introducing projects."

Not:
"Really clean homepage."

Observations preserve reasoning better than subjective opinions.

**Status**
🔒 Locked

---

### Decision
Use AI for organization—not taste.

**Why**
AI can categorize examples, identify recurring patterns, compare approaches, and retrieve references.

I remain responsible for deciding:
- what resonates
- what feels authentic
- what aligns with my design philosophy
- what belongs in my portfolio

Design taste is a human responsibility.

**Status**
🔒 Locked

## Reflection

Today's biggest realization was that the goal is not to create increasingly compressed summaries of my work.

The goal is to build a system where original research, inspiration, and design decisions remain accessible throughout the project.

AI should help me retrieve, organize, compare, and challenge ideas—not replace my judgment or design taste.
