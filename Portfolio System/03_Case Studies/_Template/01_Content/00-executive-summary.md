## Guiding Questions
1. Who was this for (company/sponsor), and what was your title/role on it?
2. In one sentence, what was the core problem or opportunity?
3. In one sentence, what did you (or the team) do about it?
4. What's the single strongest proof point — a result, a stat, a stakeholder reaction — that earns the click into the full story?
5. Is there a hero visual for this project? (Per the locked template: a hero image in the title block, plus a conditional live-site button if a real URL exists.)
