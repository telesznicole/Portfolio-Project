## Revision History

This section required real rethinking of what actually counts as proof here, not just wording polish:

- **An early draft led with traffic numbers** (a soft before/after: "the old site rarely got visited" / "73 views on the day I posted"). Nicole raised a genuine, substantive objection: leading with view counts framed success as attention, which isn't actually this project's throughline (depth and craft were). The finding order was flipped, not just softened, demoting traffic to a smaller supporting note rather than the lead proof point.
- **The traffic framing also needed a real factual correction**: an earlier version said traffic "hasn't stayed at that level since," which wasn't accurate. Nicole clarified it has stayed meaningfully higher, not dropped back down, even accounting for an honest caveat (active job searching, though that was also true before the rebuild, so it isn't the full explanation). The final version reflects the real, sustained increase.
- **The specific pre-rebuild number (0 to 4 views a month) was cut entirely**, not just softened, after Nicole said it genuinely didn't sit well with her to frame the old site as barely visited.
- **The "system already shipped real work" finding went through a real logic problem, caught directly by Nicole, not just a wording objection.** An early version cited Feminist UX's case study as external proof this system works. Nicole pointed out this doesn't actually hold together logically within a self-contained case study, referencing a separate project as evidence inside this one is a non-sequitur, not real support. Rebuilt around a cleaner, self-evident idea instead: the case study a reader is looking at right now, built by the process it describes, is itself the proof. No external citation needed.
- **The smaller traffic note got a genuinely goofy, warm closing phrase** at Nicole's request ("Not the point, but I'm not complaining"), after an earlier, flatter version ("a nice signal, but it was never really the point") read as too dry for what she wanted there.
