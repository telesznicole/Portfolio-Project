## Your Answers / Revision History

- **Team was cut and replaced with Stack.** Solo personal project, so there is no team or sponsor to name. Stack earns the slot because this project's whole premise is tools and process.
- **Duration** went through several candidates and is a good example of a figure Claude could not verify on its own. Nicole started on Aug 3. An early "7+ weeks" was computed as if the build were still ongoing, and Nicole corrected that she had finished a few weeks earlier. Claude then used the date of her LinkedIn post (about two weeks before Sept 25) as an anchor and estimated 5 to 6 weeks, flagging that as an assumption. Nicole settled on **6 weeks**.
- **Stack** went through two rejections. "Custom Code" was rejected as vague. "Custom TypeScript" was rejected because "custom" is implied (any code you write yourself is custom). Landed on "TypeScript." MCP is named explicitly because it is the actual connection layer between Claude and Nicole's filesystem and tools, and Nicole wanted it represented rather than implied.
- **Problem** took the most iteration, and it is deliberately two-fold: a wish to show more depth and craft, plus a genuine question about how AI could fit her process. Rejected along the way: "outdated portfolio" (Nicole: "outdated is mean"), "elevate my process" (she wanted "fit into" instead), and several versions that were too long. The final wording is Nicole's own: "testing how AI could fit my process." Her curiosity and motivation are meant to run through the whole telling, and this field is the first place a reader sees them.
- **Proof** had three candidates: traffic only, a blend, or process only. Chose the blend, since it answers both halves of the Problem (the AI-assisted build answers the process question, the views answer the depth-and-craft half). "Launch day" rather than "because of the redesign" is deliberate: the spike came from Nicole's LinkedIn post, so the wording says when it happened without crediting the redesign alone.
  - **Real data behind it (from Nicole):** before the launch, roughly 0 to 4 views across different months. On launch day, 73 views in a single day, with high numbers on the following days. Since the post, about 3 to 10 views (Nicole confirmed this covers the period since posting; per-day vs per-week not stated). Nicole is also applying to jobs, which likely inflates recent traffic, so the recent figure was kept out of the box and is reserved for Outcome, where there is room for that context.
