## MAJOR PROJECTS

### Feminist UX — MFA Thesis Project
Savannah College of Art and Design (SCAD)
2025

Overview:
Feminist UX is an MFA thesis project that explores how feminist theory can be translated into practical UX methods and tools. The project introduces a framework for designers to critically examine power, context, and user diversity throughout the design process, moving beyond the assumption of neutrality in UX.

Project Goal:
Transform feminist theory into actionable UX guidance that empowers designers to create more equitable, inclusive, and human-centered digital experiences.

My Role:
Independent researcher, UX designer, interaction designer, and developer.

Project Scope:
- Conducted year-long practice-based research exploring feminist HCI, Design Justice, technofeminism, cyberfeminism, and inclusive design methodologies.
- Developed a practical Feminist UX framework that connects theoretical principles to actionable design considerations.
- Designed and built an interactive educational microsite with three primary experiences:
  - Learn: Introduces feminist UX concepts, research foundations, and framework principles.
  - Experience: Uses interactive simulations to demonstrate how design decisions can reinforce or challenge existing biases.
  - Engage: Provides a toolkit to help designers apply feminist UX principles within their own workflows.
- Created a Figma plugin/toolkit concept that translates Feminist UX principles into practical design evaluation methods.
- Validated the framework through mixed-method research sessions with 10 UX practitioners.

Key Contributions:
- Bridged academic research and practical UX application by transforming theoretical concepts into tangible design tools.
- Designed a framework focused on power, context, care, equity, and empowerment within the UX process.
- Combined research, interaction design, visual design, prototyping, and front-end development to create a complete end-to-end experience.
- Iterated based on practitioner feedback to improve clarity, usability, and practical application.

Skills Demonstrated:
- UX research
- Practice-based design research
- Interaction design
- Product thinking
- Design frameworks
- Prototyping
- Front-end development
- Educational experience design
- Inclusive and equitable design practices

Project Significance:
Feminist UX represents my design philosophy and approach: creating technology that is thoughtful, reflective, and centered around the diverse experiences of the people who use it. The project demonstrates my ability to move between theory, strategy, research, design execution, and implementation while addressing complex human-centered challenges.

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Meaningful design begins with the belief that design exists to serve the people who experience it. I approach every project by seeking to understand the diverse, complex, and contextual realities of the people I am designing for, recognizing that users are not simply audiences to solve for, but individuals whose lived experiences should shape the design process.

My approach is deeply influenced by my work in Feminist UX, which emphasizes inclusivity, intersectionality, and Design Justice principles. I believe that thoughtful design requires questioning assumptions, understanding systems of power and access, and intentionally creating experiences that support equity, care, and empowerment. Research is the foundation of my process, serving as a critical tool for understanding user needs and guiding meaningful design decisions.

I believe exceptional UX exists at the intersection of empathy, strategy, and craft. While usability and functionality are essential, strong design also considers emotional connection, visual storytelling, and the details that create memorable experiences.

My background in both UX design and engineering allows me to approach problems from multiple perspectives. My technical foundation provides insight into implementation constraints and opportunities, enabling stronger collaboration between design and development teams throughout the product lifecycle. It gives my creativity a logical foundation while allowing me to design experiences that are both imaginative and technically feasible.
