## What This Iteration Tests

This is the concept Nicole asked for directly: take the About two-column split and Selected Collaborations logo-wall she liked from `v3-asymmetric-editorial`, discard that concept's rejected hero and Work sections, and pair the salvaged elements with the validated `v3-integrated-hero` foundation instead. It also tests a specific integration question: how does the new player card fit into an *asymmetric* About layout, versus the simple side-by-side pairing tested in `v4-refined-integrated`?

## Design Rationale

**Hero and Work:** Identical to `v3-integrated-hero` (now also matching `v4-refined-integrated` and `v4-bold-typographic`'s baseline treatment) — bottom-anchored credibility, logo-paired Work titles, no changes. This concept isn't testing those sections; it's isolated to About and Collaborations by design, per Nicole's specific request.

**About:** The salvaged two-column split now has the player card living in the narrower left column *alongside* the pull-quote — stacked vertically, both sitting in the same column — while the wider right column holds the bio paragraph, quick facts, and links. This is a genuinely different integration than `v4-refined-integrated`'s simple pairing: here, the player card and quote form one cohesive "about the person" column, visually distinct from the "how I think" column beside it, rather than a card floating next to a single block of mixed content.

**Selected Collaborations:** The logo-wall grid, carried over from `v3-asymmetric-editorial` largely as originally built — this concept exists partly to let that treatment be judged on its own merits, paired with content Nicole has already responded well to, rather than bundled with the hero/Work treatment she rejected it alongside last time.

## Strengths

- The most literal, direct fulfillment of a specific request Nicole made — this isn't an interpretation, it's the exact recombination she described.
- Gives the logo-wall Collaborations treatment a fair, clean evaluation for the first time — previously it was only ever seen attached to a hero and Work section she disliked for unrelated reasons.
- The player card + quote pairing in one column is a genuinely different relationship between those two elements than `v4-refined-integrated` tests, giving real signal on which integration reads better.

## Weaknesses

- Because the hero and Work sections are identical to the other two round 4 concepts, this concept's real point of differentiation is narrower — it's testing About and Collaborations specifically, not offering a full alternative page.
- The asymmetric column ratio (1fr / 1.4fr) means the player card and quote column is noticeably narrower than in `v4-refined-integrated` — worth checking the player card doesn't feel cramped at that width once real content (an actual photo, a real name) replaces the placeholders.
- Two different Selected Collaborations treatments (row-list in `v4-refined-integrated`, logo-wall here and in `v4-bold-typographic`) are now live across the round 4 set — useful for comparison, but means a final decision on that specific section is still open going into whatever comes after this round.

## Research Informing This Direction

- **Nicole's round 3 design review (this session):** the direct and explicit source for this entire concept's premise — this is closer to an instruction than an interpretation.
- **`v3-asymmetric-editorial`'s original notes:** the source of the salvaged About split and logo-wall patterns being re-tested here in their intended new context.

## Who This Serves Best

Same core audience as the validated hero/Work foundation — this concept's real test is whether the About/Collaborations treatment specifically reads as more considered to Nicole herself, not a different funnel persona.

## Outcome

**Status: Discarded**, per Nicole's round 4 design review (August 2026).

**Feedback:** "The only thing I like from this is the horizontal display of the selected collaborations, but I think I mentioned that elsewhere. Otherwise we can ditch this."

The asymmetric About split (player card + quote sharing a narrow left column) is discarded — the simple side-by-side pairing from `v4-refined-integrated` is the surviving About treatment going forward, enlarged per round 5 feedback. The logo-wall Collaborations treatment tested here survives, but was independently confirmed via `v4-bold-typographic` in the same review, so nothing unique to this concept carries forward on its own.
