## What This Iteration Tests

Whether small, quiet section titles still hold up now that everything around them has changed — more breathing room, no divider lines, an enlarged player card, a spread stats banner. Round 4's comparison between bold and quiet titles happened before any of those other fixes existed; this retests the quiet option inside the fully-refined page, since a page that's changed this much deserves a fair, current comparison rather than a conclusion carried over from an earlier, cruder version.

## Design Rationale

Every element in this concept is identical to `v5-grid-refined` except the section title treatment: `.eyebrow` here is a 13px, letter-spaced, medium-weight label — restrained, but slightly more considered than the very first round's plain eyebrow (a bit larger, a bit heavier) so it doesn't feel like a regression, just a different register. The Work layout intentionally stays as the already-validated stacked feature blocks rather than adopting either new grid or full-bleed treatment, so this concept answers exactly one question — bold titles or quiet — without mixing in the separate Work-layout decision.

## Strengths

- Isolates the title-scale decision as cleanly as this medium allows, which is exactly what Nicole asked to be able to compare.
- Demonstrates that the breathing-room and spacing fixes aren't dependent on bold titles to feel considered — if this reads as calm and confident rather than plain, it's good evidence the pacing changes are doing real work on their own.
- Lower risk than either bold-title concept if Nicole's read on "big, bold, fun" softens once she sees it fully assembled rather than described.

## Weaknesses

- Doesn't advance either of the two specifically-requested new Work layouts — deliberately, to keep this a clean comparison, but it means this concept alone doesn't answer that separate open question.
- Given how positively Nicole responded to the bold direction in round 4 ("I really love... I like the direction... I think I like this direction more than small titles"), this concept may mostly serve as confirmation rather than a live contender — worth treating it that way rather than as a coin-flip option.

## Research Informing This Direction

- **Nicole's round 4 design review (this session):** direct source — an explicit request to keep this comparison alive rather than treat round 4's verdict as final.

## Who This Serves Best

Not persona-differentiated from the other two round 5 concepts — this one exists to answer a specific open question Nicole is still weighing personally, not to serve a different audience.
