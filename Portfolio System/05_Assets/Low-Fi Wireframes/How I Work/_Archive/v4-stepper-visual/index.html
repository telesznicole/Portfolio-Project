Philosophy

How I Work

What I actually believe about design, and where it's been tested.

Meta

Design and engineering are the same conversation.

I started in software engineering before design, and I don't treat feasibility as a limit on creativity — I treat technical understanding as another design material. On the Meta engagement, that meant presenting interaction concepts fluent in both design rationale and implementation reality.

[Meta — Interaction Design Lead]
TD

Systems matter more than screens.

I rarely evaluate a decision in isolation — I'm asking what it assumes and what happens next. Leading the Terra Dotta redesign meant mapping the full student journey before touching a single page.

[Terra Dotta — Website Redesign]
Ind.

Design begins with people, not neutrality.

My Feminist UX thesis exists because "usable" isn't the same as "equitable" — a framework for designing with power, access, and context in mind, validated with 10 UX practitioners, not just my own read on it.

[Feminist UX — MFA Thesis]
FINRA

Research exists to challenge me, not confirm me.

On the FINRA engagement, that meant conducting user research and iterating through usability testing before committing to a design — research as a way to be proven wrong early, not a formality.

[FINRA — Accessibility Resource]
Hack iX

AI amplifies. It doesn't decide.

I use AI to accelerate exploration and remove repetitive work, including on this site — but every layout, every cut idea, every word was mine to make and defend. At Hack iX, that meant using AI-assisted development to prototype fast without letting the tool make the calls that were actually mine.

[Hack iX — Founding PM]
