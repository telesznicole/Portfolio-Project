Philosophy

How I Work

What I actually believe about design. Every principle is here in full — click any of them to see where it's been tested.

Design and engineering are the same conversation.

I started in software engineering before design, and I don't treat feasibility as a limit on creativity — I treat technical understanding as another design material.

Meta

On the Meta engagement, that meant presenting interaction concepts fluent in both design rationale and implementation reality — not leaving the "how" for someone else to sort out later.

See the full case study →

Systems matter more than screens.

I rarely evaluate a decision in isolation — I'm asking what it assumes and what happens next. A coherent system beats a polished screen that doesn't fit into one.

Terra Dotta

Leading the Terra Dotta redesign meant mapping the full student journey before touching a single page.

See the full case study →

Design begins with people, not neutrality.

"Usable" isn't the same as "equitable." I design with power, access, and context in mind, not an assumed neutral user who looks like everyone else.

Ind.

My Feminist UX thesis turns this into a practical framework, validated with 10 UX practitioners — not just my own read on it.

See the full case study →

Research exists to challenge me, not confirm me.

Research is how I get proven wrong early, before a user has to experience the mistake — not a formality on the way to an idea I'd already decided on.

On the FINRA engagement, that meant conducting user research and iterating through usability testing before committing to a direction.

AI amplifies. It doesn't decide.

I use AI to accelerate exploration and remove repetitive work — never to make the creative call. Every layout, every cut idea, every word stays mine to make and defend.

At Hack iX, that meant using AI-assisted development to prototype fast without letting the tool make the calls that were actually mine — including how this site itself got built.
