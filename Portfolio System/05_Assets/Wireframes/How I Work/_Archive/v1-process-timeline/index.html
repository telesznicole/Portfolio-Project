Process

How I Work

A step-by-step look at how this portfolio itself got built — discovery through build, including the parts that got rewritten along the way.

01
Discovery

Before opening a design tool, I completed deep research across hiring expectations, portfolio strategy, AI-assisted workflows, and interaction design — evidence before aesthetics, not after.

02
Foundations

A design manifesto, a portfolio PRD, and a full information architecture were written and locked before any visual exploration began. The sequence itself was a deliberate decision, not a default.

03
Design Exploration

Dozens of real portfolios were studied and organized by design problem rather than by designer, so the research stayed retrievable — for me and for AI collaboration — and answered questions like "how should a homepage work," not "what did designer X do."

04
Wireframing

Each page — Homepage, Case Study template, Resume, this one — went through multiple full rounds of AI-generated concepts, reviewed and either advanced, refined, or archived with a written reason. The homepage took nine rounds; the case study template took seven. Nothing discarded was deleted.

05
Build

Wireframes are built directly in HTML and CSS rather than a separate design tool, so structural and feasibility questions get answered immediately instead of deferred to a handoff. What you're reading right now is that same process, one page further along.
