Philosophy

How I Work

What I actually believe about design, and where it's been tested.

Meta

Design and engineering are the same conversation.

I started in software engineering before design, and I don't treat feasibility as a limit on creativity. On the Meta engagement, that meant presenting interaction concepts fluent in both design rationale and implementation reality.

TD

Systems matter more than screens.

I rarely evaluate a decision in isolation. Leading the Terra Dotta redesign meant mapping the full student journey before touching a single page — a coherent system beats a polished screen that doesn't fit into one.

Ind.

Design begins with people, not neutrality.

My Feminist UX thesis exists because "usable" isn't the same as "equitable" — a framework for designing with power, access, and context in mind, validated with 10 UX practitioners.

FINRA

Research exists to challenge me, not confirm me.

On the FINRA engagement, that meant conducting user research and iterating through usability testing before committing to a design — research as a way to be proven wrong early.

Hack iX

AI amplifies. It doesn't decide.

I use AI to accelerate exploration, including on this site — but every layout, every cut idea, every word was mine to make and defend. At Hack iX, that meant AI-assisted development without letting the tool make the calls that were actually mine.
