How I Work

Five things I actually believe about working with AI. Click to go deeper.

I use AI to speed up exploration and remove repetitive work — never to make the creative call. Every choice on this site was mine to make and defend.

The loop is always the same: I give the input, AI proposes a draft, I critique it, AI revises. First passes are starting points, never the finish line.

This project keeps a running decision log of what was tried and discarded, not just what worked. The homepage alone took nine rounds — all still documented.

Research, critique, and drafting don't come from the same place. Keeping them separate keeps any one tool from doing a job it isn't suited for.

If a decision doesn't hold up when I explain it, it doesn't ship — regardless of which tool proposed it first.
