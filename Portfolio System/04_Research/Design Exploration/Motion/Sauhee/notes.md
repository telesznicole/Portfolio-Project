Why I saved this:
Really cool use of motion and animation as you scroll through the page. It isn't overly busy or in your face, but it adds a little bit of a dynamic touch to something that could otherwise feel a bit heavy.

---

Things I like:
- Center text from hero moves to side when scrolling down to more info
- Side text changes depending on how far you go
- Use of custom pictures of herself to coincide with text

---

Things I don't like:
- A bit too much text for a home page
