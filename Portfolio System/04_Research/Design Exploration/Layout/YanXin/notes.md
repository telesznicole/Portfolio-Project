Why I saved this:
The page itself is rather busy and flashy (he does it in an incredibly cohesive yet ecclectic way that I really like) yet it feels simple and easy to follow because of how he clearly lays out and sections the flow. It is extremely simple in what things he chooses to put on there, but each choice is then made flashy and fun which lets it be easy to digest and navigate while also being exciting to look through. It shows a ton of personality. Even though I'm not sure it's my exact style (although I would love if I could emulate this somehow), I think this is absolutely my favorite homepage I've seen so far.

---

Things I like:
- Inclusion of video gives more personal way to showcase him, custom cursor on hover is youtube play button
- Order of hero then intro then video then work feels so natural in its progression
- Showcases featured work but gives way to see some past work (has a “view all work” button at the end that changes to a cheeky “yes there’s more” button on hover)
- Showcases all the brands he’s worked for, some pics from each
- Super cool loading screen of home page building in with fun items that represent him before finally showing the main title text in the hero, the items flashing in to build the title as it loads almost mirror the shapes of the letters that will be showing up there
