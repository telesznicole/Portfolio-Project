Why I saved this:
She has a very strong sense of design direction, even while walking through her case study. Her walkthrough is incredibly thorough and has a good use of visuals paired along with text. She has a strong voice in her writing that converys realness and personality without losing professinalism.

---

Things I like:
- Very strong and clean hero that works well with her stripped back personal branding in the nav bar
- Showcase of images of solution and design system right away after giving project overview
- Dynamic sidebar
- Custom mouse turns down opacity when hovering over interactive elements

---

Things I don't like:
- The different sections don't have enough breathing room between them and are hard to differentiate quickly
- Hero is stunning but requires a scroll to get any information about the project itself
