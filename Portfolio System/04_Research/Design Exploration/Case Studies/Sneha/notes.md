Why I saved this:
This gives a really good case study with clean and clear flow throughout the information. The design itself is very clean and easy to digest, but the addition of a dynamic progression bar to walk that updates while you navigate the page really helps keeps you grounded and helps you not feel lost or overwhlemed by the information being presented.

---

Things I like:
- Good use of white space to make project presentation not seem overwhelming
- Information being presented is never huge, always feels easy to dsigest (good use of hierarchy)
- Good presentation of testing metrics

---

Things I don't like:
- Some visuals (charts, diagrams) present too much information and are hard to see
- Testing metrics seem to be just thrown in at the end (no meaning attached)
- Certain elements aren't my favorite for the visual style
