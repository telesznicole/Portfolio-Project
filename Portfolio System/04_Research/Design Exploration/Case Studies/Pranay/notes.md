Why I saved this:
This case study shows a project I also worked on but does a good job of showing details for a project that is restricted by an NDA. It reuses a lot of the custom assets we created for the project which makes it feel very authentic and unique.

---

Things I like:
- Uses custom assets from project, making it feel unique and have a strong sense of brand identity
- Shows slideshow of extra images at the end (not just product but process and BTS)

---

Things I don't like:
- Doesn't give much of an insight into the process
- Strong brand identity within the project clashes strongly against the branding of his own site (especially when the big footer shows at the bottom)

---

Ideas this sparked:
- BTS images or footage to make it feel personal
- Having site branding that is clean enough to make project branding not feel like it clashes
- Have a version of the NDA protected project for general audiences but then allow a password to unlock the more full and complete version for recruiters/interviews
