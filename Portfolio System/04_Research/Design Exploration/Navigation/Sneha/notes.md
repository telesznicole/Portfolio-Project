Why I saved this:
This gives a really good case study with clean and clear flow throughout the information. The design itself is very clean and easy to digest, but the addition of a dynamic progression bar to walk that updates while you navigate the page really helps keeps you grounded and helps you not feel lost or overwhlemed by the information being presented.

---

Things I like:
- Dynamic sidebar showing page progression
- Good use of imagery to demonstrate product and process
- Strong hero that conveys the most important information right away

---

Things I don't like:
- Hero exceds viewport and requires scroll (awkward amount of bleed over viewport)
- Chart that requires sidescroll is clunky for navigation
- Title for page doesn't feel meaningful yet takes up a huge amoount of visual real estate

---

Ideas this sparked:
- Hero being a conveyer of important information (but being easy to scan)
- Dynamic sidebar (show the user where they are)
